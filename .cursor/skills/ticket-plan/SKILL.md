  *-test.plan.md, a builder plan, or a tester plan for a ticket.

# Ticket plan

- Put `*.plan.md` and `*-test.plan.md` in `.cursor/tickets/PIN-xxx/`, next to that ticket's `prompt.md`.
- Do not put them in `.cursor/plans/`.
